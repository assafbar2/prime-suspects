About Prime Suspects

Prime Suspects is a daily detective puzzle where the suspects are numbers. Twelve stand in a lineup; one is the culprit. Choose six question cards and spend up to five probes. The hidden number answers truthfully, and every suspect that disagrees leaves the room.

How a case plays

Learn with a skippable three-move teaching case. Then choose your own six-card kit, use the starter kit, or try a random selection. Ask questions, read the evidence, and make one accusation. Official daily results record solved or missed plus probes used out of five. Free endless practice never changes your daily streak.

The daily record

A new shared case opens at 00:00 UTC. Progress and the first official result for each date are saved in this browser when storage is available. You can resume unfinished cases or replay them as unranked practice. Your streak counts consecutive solved case dates, not attendance. Browser-local records are not cheat-proof or shared across devices.

The idea

Underneath the felt and the gold lettering, this is binary search wearing a hat. The best probe is the one that splits the room closest to in half, and the game rewards that instinct without ever naming it. It came out of a suspicion that the reason people find this kind of reasoning cold is presentation, not content — that the same puzzle framed as an interrogation is simply better company than the same puzzle framed as an exercise.

It is free, runs in your browser, and needs no account. The source is on GitHub. Read the privacy page for local saves and optional analytics.
